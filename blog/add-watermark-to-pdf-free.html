How to Add Watermark to PDF Free Online

📅 June 2025  ·  ✍️ Nexkittool Team  ·  ⏱️ 5 min read

Need to protect your PDF with a watermark? Whether it's "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", your company name, or a logo, Nexkittool lets you add professional watermarks to any PDF — completely free.

How to Add a Watermark to PDF Free

  1. Open PDF Tools — Go to nexkittool.com and click Add Watermark.
  2. Upload your PDF — Drag and drop your PDF file or click to browse.
  3. Set watermark text — Type your watermark text (e.g., "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", your name).
  4. Customize and apply — Adjust opacity, position, and size, then click Apply.
  5. Download — Get your watermarked PDF instantly.

💡 Pro Tip: Use 30–40% opacity for a subtle, professional watermark that doesn't obscure the document's content.

Why Add a Watermark to a PDF?

  • Copyright protection — Prevent unauthorized copying or distribution
  • Document status — Mark documents as DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, or APPROVED
  • Branding — Add your company name or logo to official documents
  • Client work — Watermark proofs sent to clients before final payment
  • Legal protection — Mark contracts and sensitive documents as confidential

Can I Remove a Watermark After Adding It?

Once a watermark is burned into a PDF, it becomes part of the document. Always keep a copy of the original unwatermarked PDF before applying a watermark.

Can I Add Multiple Watermarks?

Yes — you can apply one watermark at a time. For multiple watermarks, apply one, download, then re-upload and add another.

What Types of Watermarks Can I Add?

  • Text watermarks — Any text, custom font size, opacity, and angle
  • Diagonal watermarks — Standard 45-degree angle across each page
  • Page-wide watermarks — Full page coverage for maximum protection
