Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Open AI Content Writer — Visit nexkittool.com and click AI Writer in the AI Writing section.
  2. Enter your topic — Type in what you want to write about. Be specific: 'Benefits of drinking green tea for weight loss' works better than just 'green tea'.

Is AI-Generated Content Good for SEO?

AI content is a great starting point, but the best results come from a human-AI collaboration. Generate a draft with AI, then add your personal experience, original data, and a unique angle. Google values helpfulness and originality — AI gives you the structure and speed; you bring the authenticity. Always review and customize AI-generated content before publishing.
