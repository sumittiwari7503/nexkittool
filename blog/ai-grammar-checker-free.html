What Does an AI Grammar Checker Fix?

Modern AI grammar tools go far beyond basic spellcheck. Here's what Nexkittool's grammar checker can fix:

  • Spelling mistakes — Typos and misspelled words
  • Grammar errors — Subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, wrong word usage
  • Punctuation — Missing commas, incorrect apostrophes, wrong quotation marks
  • Sentence structure — Run-on sentences, fragments, awkward phrasing
  • Style improvements — Passive voice, wordiness, clarity suggestions

Grammar Checker vs. Spellcheck — What's the Difference?

Basic spellcheck (like in Microsoft Word) only catches misspelled words. An AI grammar checker understands context — it knows that "there", "their", and "they're" are all spelled correctly, but only one is right in a given sentence. AI tools also catch complex errors that traditional spellcheck misses completely.
