Does Paraphrasing Count as Plagiarism?

Paraphrasing source material without proper citation can still be considered plagiarism. Always cite your sources when paraphrasing academic or professional content. Use the paraphraser to improve your own writing style, not to copy others' work.

How is AI Paraphrasing Different from Spinning?

Old "article spinning" tools simply replaced words with synonyms — the results were often unreadable. AI paraphrasing powered by Google Gemini understands meaning and context. It rewrites entire sentences and paragraph structures to produce genuinely natural, readable text.
