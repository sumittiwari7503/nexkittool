To ensure your content reads naturally and remains unique, watch out for these pitfalls:

• Failing to Check for Errors: Changing sentence structures can sometimes introduce subtle grammar slips. Always run your rewritten text through our Grammar Checker to polish the final draft.
• Neglecting Originality Reviews: Always verify that the paraphrased content is original. If your paragraph is too close to the source, adjust the phrasing manually.
