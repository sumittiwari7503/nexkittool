📅 June 2025  ·  ✍️ Nexkittool Team  ·  ⏱️ 4 min read

If you've ever tried to email a PDF and seen the dreaded "file too large" error, you know the frustration. Large PDFs are slow to upload, hard to share, and take up unnecessary storage space. Why PDF Files Get So Large

PDF files become bloated for several reasons. High-resolution images embedded in the document are the biggest culprit — a single photo can add several megabytes. Fonts, metadata, form fields, and embedded attachments also add to the size. When you compress a PDF, the tool optimizes these elements without visibly degrading the document's appearance.
