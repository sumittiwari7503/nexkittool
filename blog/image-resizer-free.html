How to Resize an Image Free on Nexkittool

  1. Go to Image Resizer — Visit nexkittool.com and select Image Resizer.
  2. Upload your image — Supports JPG, PNG, WEBP, and BMP formats.
  3. Enter new dimensions — Type in exact width and height in pixels, or enter a percentage (e.g., 50% to halve the size). Lock the aspect ratio to avoid stretching.

  5. Download — Save your resized image. Choose your preferred output format (JPG or PNG).

💡 Pro Tip: Always enable "Lock Aspect Ratio" unless you specifically need a distorted stretch. This ensures your image looks natural at any size.

Resize by Pixels vs Percentage — Which to Use?

Use pixels when you need an exact size (like a 1080×1080 Instagram post). Use percentage when you want to scale an image proportionally — for example, reduce to 50% of the original to halve both dimensions while keeping the same shape.

Does Resizing Reduce Image Quality?

Enlarging an image (making it bigger) always reduces quality somewhat, since you're adding pixels that weren't there. Reducing an image (making it smaller) generally preserves sharpness very well. Nexkittool uses high-quality algorithms to minimize quality loss in both cases.
