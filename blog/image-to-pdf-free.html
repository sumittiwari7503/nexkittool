Need to send multiple photos as a single document? Or convert a scanned image into a proper PDF? Nexkittool converts any image — or group of images — into a PDF file for free, with no software installation needed.

How to Convert Image to PDF Free

  1. Open PDF Tools — Go to nexkittool.com and click Image to PDF.
  2. Upload images — Select one or multiple images (JPG, PNG, WEBP). Each image becomes one page in the PDF.
  3. Arrange order — Drag images into the order you want them in the PDF.
  4. Convert and Download — Click Convert to PDF and download your file instantly.

Common Uses for Image to PDF Conversion

  • Scanned documents — Convert phone photos of documents into proper PDF files
  • Photo albums — Combine holiday photos into a PDF slideshow
  • Certificates and ID cards — Submit scanned images as PDF for applications
  • Receipts and invoices — Photograph receipts and convert them to PDF for accounting
  • Assignment submission — Convert handwritten notes photos into a PDF

Which Image Formats are Supported?

Nexkittool supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, and BMP formats for conversion to PDF. You can mix different image formats in the same PDF.

How Many Images Can I Convert at Once?

You can convert up to 30 images into a single PDF for free. Each image becomes one page in the resulting PDF document.

Will My Image Quality be Preserved?

Yes. Nexkittool embeds your original images into the PDF without compressing or reducing their quality. The PDF will look exactly as sharp as your original images.
