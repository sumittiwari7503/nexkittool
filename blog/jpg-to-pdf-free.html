Why Convert JPG to PDF?

PDFs are the universal format for sharing documents. When you send a JPG, the receiver's screen size or software can display it differently. A PDF looks identical on every device. That's why resumes, assignments, invoices, and official documents are almost always submitted as PDFs — not JPGs.

  • PDFs are smaller and easier to email than multiple JPG files
  • You can combine multiple JPG images into one PDF document
  • PDF preserves the exact layout and image quality
  • Most offices and schools require PDF submissions

JPG vs PDF — Which Format Should You Use?

Use JPG when sharing photos on social media or when the image itself is the final product. Use PDF when submitting documents, assignments, invoices, or anything professional. A PDF created from JPG retains full image quality but also adds the benefits of document formatting, page numbers, and universal compatibility.
