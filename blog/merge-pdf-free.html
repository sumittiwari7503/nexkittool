Why Combine Multiple PDFs Into One Document?

Packaging your separate files into a single PDF document offers several advantages for personal and business tasks:
• Simplify Emailing: Send a single, organized attachment instead of hitting email size caps with loose files.
• Improve Document Flow: Keep paragraphs, lists, and images in a consistent sequence so readers review them in order.
• Reduce Storage Waste: Merging files can strip duplicate metadata fields, slightly optimizing file storage footprints.

Preserving Document Hierarchy, Hyperlinks, and Outlines

A common problem with low-quality PDF compilers is that they strip internal links, bookmarks, and structural layers. Common Mistakes to Avoid When Merging PDFs

• Merging Encrypted Files: Password-protected files will fail during compilation. Unlock files using our PDF unlocker before uploading.
• Ignoring mixed page layouts: Combining portrait pages with landscape slides can break formatting flow. Check alignments in the editor thumbnails.
