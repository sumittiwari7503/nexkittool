How to Convert PDF to Excel Free Online — Extract Tables Instantly

📅 June 13, 2025  ·  ✍️ Nexkittool Team  ·  ⏱️ 5 min read

When data is stuck in a PDF, it's frustrating — you can see the numbers but can't work with them. Converting PDF to Excel extracts tables and data into a fully editable spreadsheet, so you can sort, calculate, and analyze without tedious manual retyping. Nexkittool does it free, online, in seconds.

When Do You Need PDF to Excel?

  • Extract financial data from bank statements or reports
  • Convert invoice tables into editable spreadsheets
  • Import sales data from PDF reports into Excel for analysis
  • Extract survey results from PDF to calculate in Excel
  • Convert government data tables from PDF to XLS

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Open PDF to Excel Tool — Go to nexkittool.com and select PDF to Excel.
  2. Upload your PDF — Upload the PDF containing the table or data you want to extract.
  3. Select pages (optional) — Specify which pages contain the tables if you don't need the full document converted.
  4. Click Convert — Nexkittool extracts the tables and converts them to Excel format.
  5. Download your Excel file — Download the .xlsx file and open it directly in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

💡 Pro Tip: PDFs with clear, well-formatted tables give the best conversion results. Scanned PDFs (image-based) require OCR processing for accurate extraction.

How Accurate Is PDF to Excel Conversion?

Conversion accuracy depends on how the original PDF was created. Digitally-created PDFs (from Excel, Word, or software) convert with near-perfect accuracy — tables are extracted cleanly. Scanned PDFs (photographs of documents) use OCR technology and are generally accurate for clear, high-contrast scans but may need light editing for complex layouts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Excel?

Yes. Nexkittool uses OCR to read scanned PDFs and extract tables into Excel. Quality depends on scan clarity.

Does it preserve formatting like borders and merged cells?

Basic formatting is preserved. Complex merged cells and nested tables may need minor adjustment after conversion.

What if my PDF has multiple tables?

All tables on the selected pages are extracted and placed into separate sheets in the Excel file.
