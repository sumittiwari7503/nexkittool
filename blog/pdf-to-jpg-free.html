How to Convert PDF to JPG Free Online — Extract Pages as Images

📅 June 2025  ·  ✍️ Nexkittool Team  ·  ⏱️ 4 min read

Need to extract a page from a PDF as an image? Or convert an entire PDF document into individual JPG files? Converting PDF to JPG is useful for presentations, social media posts, thumbnails, and anywhere you need an image instead of a document. Best of all, you can do it completely free online — no Adobe Acrobat needed.

Why Would You Convert PDF to JPG?

  • Share a PDF page on Instagram, WhatsApp, or social media (which don't support PDFs)
  • Extract a specific chart, table, or infographic from a PDF as an image
  • Create preview thumbnails for PDF documents
  • Use a PDF page as a background or in a presentation
  • Embed a PDF page into a Word document or website

How to Convert PDF to JPG Free on Nexkittool

  1. Open Nexkittool — Go to nexkittool.com and select PDF to JPG from the PDF Tools section.
  2. Upload your PDF — Click to upload or drag and drop your PDF file. Files up to 100MB are supported.
  3. Select pages — Choose to convert all pages or specific page numbers only.
  4. Convert — Hit the Convert button. Each page becomes a separate high-quality JPG image.
  5. Download — Download individual images or all pages as a ZIP file at once.

💡 Pro Tip: For best quality, choose the highest DPI setting available. 300 DPI is ideal for printing; 150 DPI is fine for screen use.

What Image Quality Will I Get?

Nexkittool converts PDF pages to JPG at high resolution, preserving text sharpness and image quality. The output is suitable for professional use, presentations, and printing. Text remains crisp and readable even at high zoom levels.

Can I Convert a Specific Page Only?

Yes. When you upload your PDF, you can specify exactly which pages to convert. This saves time if you only need one or two pages from a 50-page document.

Frequently Asked Questions

You'll need to remove the password protection first. Nexkittool has an Unlock PDF tool for this — then you can convert to JPG.
