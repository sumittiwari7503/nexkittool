Why Would You Need to Convert PDF to Word?

  • Edit a contract or official document you received as PDF

Does Formatting Get Preserved?

Yes — Nexkittool's converter preserves text, headings, tables, lists, and most formatting. Complex multi-column layouts may need minor adjustments, but all your content will be correctly extracted and editable.

Is It Safe to Upload My PDF?

100% safe. Your files are processed securely and automatically deleted after conversion. We never store or share your documents.

What's the Difference Between PDF to Word and OCR?

Regular PDF to Word conversion works on text-based PDFs (where text can be selected). OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is needed for scanned PDFs — images of documents where the text isn't selectable. Nexkittool handles both types automatically.
