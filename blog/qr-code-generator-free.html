How to Create a QR Code for Free – Complete Guide 2025

📅 June 2025  ·  ✍️ Nexkittool Team  ·  ⏱️ 4 min read

QR codes are everywhere — restaurant menus, business cards, product packaging, event tickets, and payment systems. Creating your own QR code is completely free and takes less than 10 seconds. Here's everything you need to know.

What is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that can be scanned by any smartphone camera. It can store various types of information — website URLs, contact details, text, email addresses, phone numbers, and more. When someone scans it, their phone instantly opens the stored information.

  2. Enter your content — Type or paste the URL, text, phone number, or any information you want to encode.

  4. Download PNG — Download your QR code as a high-quality PNG image ready to print or share.

💡 Pro Tip: For business use, always link your QR code to a URL you control, so you can update the destination anytime without reprinting.

What Can You Use QR Codes For?

  • Business cards — Link to your portfolio, LinkedIn, or website
  • Restaurant menus — Replace physical menus with a digital version
  • Product packaging — Link to tutorials, warranties, or product details
  • Event posters — Quick registration or ticket purchase links
  • Payments — Link to UPI payment pages or PayPal
  • WiFi sharing — Let guests connect to WiFi by scanning a code

Are QR Codes Free to Use Forever?

QR codes generated on Nexkittool are static QR codes — they are completely free and work forever with no expiry. Dynamic QR codes (which can be edited after creation) require paid services, but for most uses, static QR codes work perfectly.
