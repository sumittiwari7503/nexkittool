How to Split PDF Free Online — Extract Any Pages Instantly

Have a 50-page PDF but only need pages 10 to 15? Or want to split a large report into separate chapters? Splitting a PDF is something millions of people do every day — and you don't need expensive software to do it. Nexkittool lets you split any PDF for free, right in your browser.

When Do You Need to Split a PDF?

  • Extract specific pages from a large report or book
  • Separate a combined invoice into individual bills
  • Share only a part of a confidential document
  • Break a large PDF into smaller files for emailing
  • Extract a single chapter from an ebook or manual

How to Split PDF Free on Nexkittool

  1. Open Split PDF Tool — Visit nexkittool.com and click on Split PDF in the PDF Tools section.
  2. Upload your PDF — Drag and drop or click to upload. Works with files up to 100MB.
  3. Choose split mode — Split into individual pages, by page range (e.g., pages 5–10), or split every N pages.
  4. Click Split PDF — The tool processes your document and creates the split files.
  5. Download — Download each split file individually or all together as a ZIP archive.

💡 Pro Tip: After splitting, use the Compress PDF tool to further reduce file sizes before emailing or uploading.

Split vs Extract Pages — What's the Difference?

Splitting a PDF divides it into multiple files following a set pattern — for example, every 5 pages becomes a new file. Extracting pages means picking specific page numbers (like pages 3, 7, and 12) to form a new document. Nexkittool supports both methods.

Can I Split a PDF on My Phone?

Absolutely. Nexkittool is fully responsive and works perfectly on mobile browsers. No app download required — just open the website on iPhone or Android and split your PDF in seconds.
