Forgot the password to an old PDF? Or need to edit a document someone sent you with restrictions? Unlocking a PDF lets you remove passwords and copy/print/edit restrictions — all for free online, without any software installation.

Two Types of PDF Passwords

  • Open password: Prevents anyone from opening the PDF without entering the correct password
  • Permission password: Allows the PDF to be opened but restricts actions like printing, copying text, or editing

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Open Unlock PDF Tool — Go to nexkittool.com and select Unlock PDF.
  2. Upload your locked PDF — Upload the password-protected PDF file.
  3. Enter password (if known) — If you know the open password, enter it. For permission-only locks, no password is needed.
  4. Click Unlock — The tool removes the password and restrictions from your PDF.
  5. Download unlocked PDF — Download your unlocked PDF — now fully editable, printable, and copyable.

💡 Note: You can only unlock a PDF if you are the owner or have legal authorization. Nexkittool cannot bypass unknown open passwords.

When Is It Legal to Unlock a PDF?

Unlocking a PDF you own, created yourself, or have permission to access is perfectly legal. Common legal uses include removing a password you forgot from your own document, removing print/copy restrictions from a PDF you're authorized to edit, and decrypting old business documents you created previously. Never use PDF unlocking to access documents you don't have rights to.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I unlock a PDF if I don't know the password?

Nexkittool can remove permission restrictions (print, copy, edit) without a password. Open-password protection requires the correct password.

Is the process secure?

Yes. Files are processed on secure servers and deleted immediately after. No data is stored.

What if the PDF has DRM protection?

DRM (Digital Rights Management) is different from regular PDF passwords. DRM-protected files (like ebooks from Amazon) cannot be unlocked.
