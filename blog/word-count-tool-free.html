Why Monitor Word and Character Counts?

Web platforms require specific text lengths to display copy correctly without truncation:
• SEO Meta Tags: Google search results crop metadata titles after 60 characters and descriptions after 160 characters. Keeping within these limits is vital for search rankings and snippets.
• Social Media: Platforms like Twitter/X cap posts at 280 characters, and LinkedIn limits bios to 2000 characters.
• Academic Writing: Essays, abstracts, and reports require strict word count ranges (e.g., a 500-word essay). Exceeding or falling short of these can affect grades.

Different writing software uses varying count rules. For example, Microsoft Word and Google Docs count letters, numbers, and punctuation, but handle hyphenated words or line breaks differently.
