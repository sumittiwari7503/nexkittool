Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

About Cookies: Cookies are small text files stored in your web browser. This policy explains what cookies we set, how we use third-party analytics and advertising scripts, and how you can control your preferences.

1. How We Use Cookies

Nexkittool uses cookies to ensure our tools run smoothly, remember your theme/UI preferences, secure account sessions, and analyze anonymous visitor trends. We categorize these cookies into essential and non-essential.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: These are required for fundamental site operations (e.g., maintaining account logins, managing Pro tier access states, and processing payment transactions via Stripe). These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: These allow us to remember local choices you make (such as UI theme mode, recent tools list, or tool input histories) for a more personalized experience.
  • Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics): We use Google Analytics to measure anonymized traffic and page visits so we can optimize tool speeds and user flows. These are only activated if you consent to tracking.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense): Google AdSense uses cookies to serve personalized advertisements to visitors based on their browsing behavior on our site and other internet platforms. Pro subscribers do not receive these cookies.

3. Consent & Control Options

You can manage your consent status at any time. When you first visit Nexkittool, you will see a Cookie Consent popup offering to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can also clear your local cache, block cookies entirely, or opt-out of personalized advertising via:

  • Google Ad Settings Portal
  • Your individual browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, etc.)

4. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to align with new legal requirements or tool changes. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when the policy was revised.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our cookie practices, please reach out to us at support@nexkittool.com.
