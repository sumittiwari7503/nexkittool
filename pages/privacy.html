Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Short Version: We don't sell your data. Files are deleted immediately after processing. We only collect what's necessary to provide our service.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Nexkittool, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (encrypted).
  • Files: Files you upload are processed and immediately deleted after the operation completes. We do NOT store your files permanently.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymized data about which tools are used to improve our service.
  • Payment Information: Payments are processed by Stripe. We never store your card details.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for session management and Google AdSense advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our tools and services
  • To process payments for Pro subscriptions
  • To send account-related notifications (not spam)
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. File Processing & Storage

All files uploaded to Nexkittool are:

  • Processed in a secure, isolated environment
  • Automatically deleted within 1 hour of processing (usually seconds)
  • Never analyzed, sold, or shared with third parties
  • Transmitted over HTTPS encryption

4. Google AdSense & Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your browsing. You can opt out via Google's Ad Settings. Pro users see no ads.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini AI: Powers our AI writing tools. Text inputs are processed per Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: Handles all payment processing securely.
  • Google Analytics: Anonymized usage statistics.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure server infrastructure, regular security audits, and access controls.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your data, correct inaccurate data, delete your account, export your data, and opt out of marketing communications. Contact us at privacy@nexkittool.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Nexkittool is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We'll notify registered users of significant changes via email.

10. Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Contact us at privacy@nexkittool.com or visit our Contact page.
