Protect PDF Documents with Watermarks

PDF watermarking is the process of overlaying semi-transparent text across all pages of a PDF document. This is essential for protecting confidential drafts, contracts, and intellectual property from unauthorized sharing.

How to Use the Free PDF Watermark Generator

  1. Upload PDF: Select the PDF you want to protect.
  2. Configure text: Type your watermark (e.g. "CONFIDENTIAL" or "DRAFT").
  3. Position and download: Set opacity, angle, and text size, then download the protected PDF.

Key Benefits of NexKitTool PDF Watermark Generator

  • Page-by-Page Overlay: Adds the watermark text to all pages of the document automatically.
  • Adjustable Opacity: Set text opacity so the watermark is visible without blocking page content.

Typical Use Cases

  • Marking Business Drafts: Mark proposal drafts as "DRAFT" before sharing them with clients.
  • Protecting Invoices: Watermark invoices with payment terms to prevent modification.
