Why Watermark Your Images?

Watermarking your photos helps safeguard your creative work, establish brand attribution, and discourage unauthorized sharing online. Whether you are distributing photography drafts, publishing product catalog listings, or sharing personal snapshots, adding a visible text watermark directly onto the image ensures your ownership remains recognized wherever the picture appears across the web.

The NexKitTool Add Watermark tool allows you to add custom text, such as your name, business handle, copyright notice, or website URL, to uploaded images in seconds.
