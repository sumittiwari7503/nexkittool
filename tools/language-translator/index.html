Tips for Best Results

For the most natural results, translate full sentences rather than isolated words or short phrases, since AI translation relies on surrounding context to choose the right meaning. If a translation seems off, try rephrasing the original sentence more simply before re-translating. It also helps to break up very long paragraphs into shorter ones, since AI translation models generally preserve nuance better across shorter, well-defined blocks of text than across one long unbroken passage.
