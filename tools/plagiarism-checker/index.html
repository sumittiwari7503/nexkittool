What Does This Plagiarism Checker Do?

Nexkittool's Plagiarism Checker uses Google Gemini to analyze your text and produce an originality assessment — a 0-100% rating along with an explanation of patterns that read as generic, formulaic, or potentially AI-generated.

Important distinction: this is an AI-pattern analysis tool, not a database-matching plagiarism scanner like Turnitin. It doesn't crawl billions of indexed web pages and academic papers to find an exact-match source. Instead, it evaluates writing style and originality signals using language understanding. For submissions where an institution requires a specific database-matching plagiarism report, use the tool your school or publisher officially mandates.

Who Should Use This Tool

People Also Ask About Free AI Plagiarism & Originality Checker

Is this the same as Turnitin or a database-matching plagiarism checker?

No. This tool analyzes writing patterns using AI rather than matching your text against a database of indexed web pages and academic papers. If your school or publisher requires a specific database-matching report, use their official tool.

What does the originality percentage mean?

It's an AI-generated estimate of how generic, formulaic, or original your text's phrasing appears — not a guarantee of the absence of plagiarism from any specific source.

Can this tool detect if text was written by AI?

It can flag some common AI-writing patterns as part of its analysis, though no detection method — including this one — is 100% reliable given how AI writing styles continue to evolve.

Is my submitted text stored or shared?

No, text is processed to generate your analysis and is not stored, indexed, or shared afterward.

Can I check a whole essay at once?

Yes, you can paste full essays or articles; for very long documents, checking section by section can give more detailed feedback per section.

Should I rely on this alone before submitting academic work?

Use it as a helpful first-pass self-check, but always follow your institution's official plagiarism-checking requirements for formal submissions.
