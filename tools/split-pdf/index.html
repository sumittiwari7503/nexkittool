Why Split a PDF?

Splitting a PDF allows you to extract specific page ranges or save individual pages as separate documents. This is useful when you want to chapter textbooks, separate monthly statements, or isolate legal contract blocks. Our client-side page extraction is secure and preserves text structures, interactive forms, bookmarks, and layout styles. Our secure compiler processes PDF stream layers, ensuring that fonts, margins, page boundaries, and internal object coordinates remain optimized. Frequently Asked Questions

Does splitting a PDF reduce its layout quality?

No. Splitting simply copies the original pages and puts them into a new file, maintaining text and image clarity.

Can I split a PDF into individual files?

Yes, you can choose the split-by-page option to extract every page as a separate PDF document.

Will internal links inside the PDF still work after splitting?

Yes. Internal links that point to pages within the extracted range will continue to function correctly.

Do you store a copy of my split documents?

No. All document processing happens locally in your browser memory and is deleted instantly when the tab is closed.
