Why Split a PDF?

PDF documents often contain unnecessary pages. Splitting a PDF allows you to extract only the pages you need (like a single chapter of a book or an invoice page from a larger packet), reducing file size and keeping data relevant.

How to Use the Free Split PDF Online

  1. Upload document: Select the PDF you wish to split.

  3. Split and download: Download the extracted pages instantly.
